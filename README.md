# Hide My Device

Overview
The Hide My Device app allows users of Homey Pro 2023 to hide specific devices from appearing in their Homey app. This is perfect for situations where you want to keep your device list clean and free of clutter by hiding devices you don't need to interact with regularly.

Usage:
- Install the Hide My Device app
- Go to the app settings of Hide My Device
- Select the devices you want to hide from the Homey app.
- The selected devices will no longer appear in your Homey app.
